Learning & Practice

* [FIFA](https://www.fifa.com/) - The official governing body for world football, offering news, rankings, tournament information, and the Laws of the Game.
* [UEFA](https://www.uefa.com/) - The official website for European football, featuring the UEFA Champions League, Europa League, fixtures, and statistics.
* [The Athletic Football](https://www.nytimes.com/athletic/football/) - Provides in-depth football journalism, tactical analysis, and exclusive reporting from around the world.
* [BBC Sport Football](https://www.bbc.com/sport/football) - Delivers comprehensive football news, live scores, match reports, and expert analysis.
* [Sky Sports Football](https://www.skysports.com/football) - Offers breaking football news, live match coverage, transfer updates, and video highlights.

Analysis, Study & Reference

* [Transfermarkt](https://www.transfermarkt.com/) - A leading resource for player market values, transfers, contracts, squad information, and historical statistics.
* [FBref](https://fbref.com/) - Provides detailed player, team, and competition statistics, including advanced performance metrics used by analysts.
* [Sofascore](https://www.sofascore.com/) - Features live scores, player ratings, detailed match statistics, and real-time performance tracking across football competitions.
* [FotMob](https://www.fotmob.com/) - Offers live scores, fixtures, match statistics, personalized notifications, and news for leagues worldwide.
* [WorldFootball.net](https://www.worldfootball.net/) - Maintains an extensive historical database of football competitions, clubs, players, results, and records from around the globe.
